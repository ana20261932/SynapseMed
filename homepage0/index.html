The daily reality of a South African practice

Disconnected records, slow medical-aid claims, manual inventory, and no unified view of switch billing. These aren't minor annoyances — they cost practices time, money, and patients.

Claims stuck in limbo

Medical-aid submissions take days. No real-time adjudication status. Patients are left unsure of co-payments and your cash flow suffers.

Inventory guesswork

Pharmacy stock tracked on paper or spreadsheets. NAPPI codes missing. Low-stock alerts missed. Expired batches slip through.

Practice admin overload

Appointments, clinical notes, and billing live in separate systems. No unified dashboard for the practice owner to see the full picture.

Sources: SA medical-aid switch billing, POPIA Act 4 of 2013, NAPPI pharmacy coding
